Founding framework — for member review and vote

This page is for members of the AI Whisperers Cooperative. It contains the proposed founding framework drafted after the April 30, 2026 design session and the May 5 working sync between Shannon and Randy.

Three views of the same framework. Read whichever matches how you think — they say the same thing in different formats.

Three ways to read the framework
01Design view — interactive map →

Fourteen framework sections grouped into six clusters. Hover any of the eight cooperative success patterns to see which sections produce it. Click sections to expand recommendation, reasoning, and failure mode.
02Sequence view — rollout timeline →

Six phases from vote to first 90-day review. Click any phase to see activities and acceptance criteria. Phases 3 and 4 deliberately overlap — first client work starts before the first operating cycle ends.
03Full text — votable proposal →

The complete written proposal. Fifteen sections plus rollout sequence and ballot. This is the document being voted on. The map and timeline are visual aids; this is the source of truth.
How to vote
Cast your vote →

Three options: adopt as proposed, adopt with amendments, or do not adopt. Reply by email to Shannon and Randy with your vote. Vote due seven days from circulation.
